Releases and Development Momentum
---------------------------------

The Zephyr RTOS project continues to accelerate, delivering major feature releases, winning adoption from industry giants, and celebrating a growing commit history that underscores its long-term vitality. This week's highlights include the 2.4 release expanding commercial toolchain support for product makers, the 4.4 release adding WireGuard, Wi‑Fi Direct, and OpenRISC support, and a special podcast commemorating 150,000 commits.

* `Google and Facebook Select Zephyr RTOS for Next Generation Products <https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/google-and-facebook-select-zephyr-rtos-for-next-generation-products-301141973.html>`_ (Tavily Search) — Release 2.4 adds infrastructure for commercial toolchains, boosting support for product makers.
* `Community – Zephyr Project <https://www.zephyrproject.org/community>`_ (Tavily Search) — Zephyr RTOS 4.4 arrives with WireGuard, Wi‑Fi Direct, OpenRISC, and more.
* `150,000 Commits Later — Zephyr Podcast #048 <https://zephyrproject.org/150000-commits-later-zephyr-podcast-048/>`_ (Zephyr Project) — A milestone episode marking 150k commits in main and 1,455 PRs merged in August.

A Growing Industry Footprint
----------------------------

From opinion pieces to real-world robotics deployments, Zephyr's momentum across the embedded landscape is unmistakable. Industry commentators argue the RTOS is poised to dominate the embedded world, while hardware makers such as ST explore sensor-driven humanoid robots that increasingly rely on Zephyr foundations.

* `5 Reasons Why Zephyr RTOS Will Rule the World <https://www.designnews.com/embedded-systems/zephyr-rtos-5-reasons-why-it-will-dominate-the-embedded-world-transform-industry>`_ (Tavily Search) — Makes the case for Zephyr's dominance across the embedded industry.
* `Humanoid robots: building a workforce from the sensors up <https://blog.st.com/humanoid-robots-building-a-workforce/>`_ (The ST Blog) — Explores how sensor-rich humanoid robots are built and where Zephyr fits into the stack.

New Hardware in the Ecosystem
-----------------------------

A wave of new boards and modules is expanding the Zephyr-ready hardware landscape, from NDAA-compliant drone carrier boards to high-performance AI alternatives to NVIDIA Jetson and budget-friendly single-board computers. CNX Software covered three notable arrivals this week.

* `ARK Just A Pi – A tiny NDAA-compliant Raspberry Pi CM5 carrier board for drones and robots <https://www.cnx-software.com/2026/08/31/ark-just-a-pi-a-tiny-ndaa-compliant-raspberry-pi-cm5-carrier-board-for-drones-and-robots/>`_ (CNX Software – Embedded Systems News)
* `D-Robotics RDK S100P – A 128 TOPS alternative to NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX 16GB with Cortex-A78AE/R52+ cores <https://www.cnx-software.com/2026/08/31/d-robotics-rdk-s100p-a-128-tops-alternative-to-nvidia-jetson-orin-nx-16gb-with-cortex-a78ae-r52-cores/>`_ (CNX Software – Embedded Systems News)
* `Orange Pi Zero 4 – Compact Allwinner A733 SBC offers HDMI, USB-C DP, GbE, WiFI 6, PCIe FFC connector for $26.5 and up <https://www.cnx-software.com/2026/08/31/orange-pi-zero-4-allwinner-a733-sbc-offers-hdmi-usb-c-dp-gbe-wifi-6-pcie-ffc-connector/>`_ (CNX Software – Embedded Systems News)

Community and Global Events

The Zephyr community remains as active as ever, celebrating a decade of contribution with a new awards program and gathering developers internationally. A recent meetup recap from Sapporo, Japan showcases the project's global reach, while the `community page <https://www.zephyrproject.org/community>`_ (Tavily Search) announced the new Zephyr Community Awards marking 10 years of contribution.
