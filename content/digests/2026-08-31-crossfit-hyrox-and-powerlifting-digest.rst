Competitive Fitness at a Crossroads
------------------------------------

This week's headlines paint a picture of a fitness industry in flux. From the battle for dominance between standardized racing formats like HYROX and traditional boxes like CrossFit, to financial instability plaguing longstanding event organizers, the landscape is shifting. Athletes are navigating career transitions, while brands face tough questions about sustainability and direction.
