Open-Source Hardware Digest — Week 36, 2026

The Big Picture: Open-Source Hardware's Growing Impact

This week's coverage underscores how open-source hardware is moving from hobbyist curiosity to strategic disruption. From the origins of Arduino to predictions about datacenters, and an evolving catalog of projects, the ecosystem is broadening its reach.

  • The Rise of Open-Source Hardware in Electronics Design: A Revolution in Innovation (Source: Tavily Search) — A look at Arduino's continued leadership and the expanding ecosystem of shields and libraries.
  • Open-Source Hardware: Prepare For Disruption (Source: Tavily Search) — How open-source hardware could shake up the datacenter, much as Linux changed software.
  • List of open-source hardware projects - Wikipedia (Source: Tavily Search) — A useful reference point for the breadth of open-source hardware efforts.

Hands-On Projects and Guides

For makers and engineers, this week brings a fruitful mix of practical builds and updated reference material. A battery-free NFC business card shows what can be done with energy harvesting, a detailed ESP32 pinout guide saves endless forum digging, and Adafruit refreshes its audio amplifier guide.

  • An NFC energy-harvesting PCB business card with a microcontroller (Source: Adafruit Industries – Makers, hackers, artists, designers and engineers!) — Wilson Harper's KiCad project lights up 21 LEDs using only harvested NFC energy.
  • Building An Energy-Harvesting Business Card (Source: Hackaday) — A closer look at the same clever design from the build log perspective.
  • ESP32-WROOM-32 Pinout: GPIOs, strapping pins, ADC (Source: Adafruit Industries – Makers, hackers, artists, designers and engineers!) — Avoiding the boot-failure traps by knowing which GPIOs are safe to use.
  • UPDATED GUIDE: Adafruit MAX98357 I2S Class-D Amps – Stereo and Mono #AdafruitLearningSystem @Adafruit (Source: Adafruit Industries – Makers, hackers, artists, designers and engineers!) — The refreshed guide now covers the new Adafruit Stereo I2S 3W Amplifier Breakout.

Simulation and System Design

Open-source principles are also advancing system-level engineering. This week's entries cover simulating UAVs in Unreal Engine and a heat exchanger design to defeat bacteria in hot water systems — both demonstrating how shared designs accelerate practical problem-solving.

  • Simulating UAVs In Unreal Engine (Source: Hackaday) — Exploring drone behavior in a high-fidelity virtual environment.
  • Defeating Bacteria In Hot Water Systems With A Heat Exchanger (Source: Hackaday) — An engineering approach to keeping water systems safe and efficient.
